Privacy

Last revised 2026-10-26

This site is a set of static files served by GitHub Pages. It has no backend, no database, no account system, no contact form, and no analytics.

It sets no cookies and runs no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do here is recorded by me, so there is nothing for me to store, share, sell, or hand over. Earlier versions of this site ran Google Analytics; that was removed in the 2026 rebuild and the property is no longer referenced.

Two things are worth naming because they are requests your browser makes to third parties on this site's behalf:

  • GitHub Pages serves the files, and like any web host receives your IP address and user agent in the ordinary course of serving a request. GitHub's own privacy statement governs that.
  • Google Fonts serves the two typefaces this site is set in, which means your browser requests them from fonts.gstatic.com. Google's privacy policy governs that request.

If you email me, I have your email — that is how email works. I will use it to reply to you and nothing else, and I will not add you to anything.

Questions about any of this go to Malek.Taldoss@gmail.com.
